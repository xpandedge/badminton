# Privacy and Terms Pages Design

## Goal

Publish clear, public Privacy Policy and Terms of Use pages for DuoRally, operated by Xpandedge Pty Ltd. The documents should describe the product as it exists, preserve Australian consumer rights, and give users a practical route for privacy requests and complaints.

## Legal Basis

The Privacy Policy follows the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles where they apply. Its structure covers the topics identified by the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, including collection, use, disclosure, overseas handling, security, access, correction and complaints. Marketing commitments follow the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

The Terms preserve rights and remedies that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law in Schedule 2 to the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth). Queensland law is the governing jurisdiction, subject to any mandatory law that applies elsewhere.

## Routes and Access

- `/privacy` hosts the Privacy Policy.
- `/terms` hosts the Terms of Use.
- Both routes are public and require no DuoRally account.
- Landing, sign-in and Help surfaces link to both documents.
- Sign-in and registration state that continuing means agreeing to the Terms and acknowledging the Privacy Policy.
- Both pages display `Last updated: 9 August 2026`.

## Privacy Policy Content

The policy identifies Xpandedge Pty Ltd as operator and `contact@xpandedge.com.au` as the contact for privacy requests, access, correction and complaints.

It describes the information DuoRally handles:

- account identity, email and profile details
- group membership and roles
- guest names and skill levels entered by organisers
- RSVPs, attendance, match assignments, scores and player statistics
- venue, court and session information
- technical, device and product-usage information
- messages sent to Xpandedge for support or privacy requests

It explains collection sources and purposes, service-provider disclosures, overseas processing, security, retention, account/data requests, complaints, marketing consent and unsubscribe handling, data-breach response, guest-player responsibilities and policy changes.

## Terms Content

The Terms cover:

- acceptance and eligibility
- account security and accurate information
- Owner, Admin and Member responsibilities
- organiser responsibility for guest information, venues, courts and session decisions
- fair scheduling as a decision-support feature rather than a guarantee of perfect equality
- player responsibility for participation, health, safety and venue rules
- acceptable use
- licence to process user-submitted content only as needed to operate DuoRally
- Xpandedge intellectual property
- availability, product changes, suspension and termination
- liability limitations that do not exclude Australian Consumer Law rights
- governing law, complaints and contact details

## Experience

The pages use a shared legal-document layout consistent with DuoRally: a compact dark header, restrained content column, visible section navigation and high-contrast typography. They remain easy to scan on a phone, print cleanly and avoid card-heavy presentation.

The legal links are intentionally quiet but consistently available. They should not compete with the primary app workflow.
